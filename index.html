About North American Logistics

Freight covered. On time. Priced right.

North American Logistics is a full truckload brokerage covering freight across the U.S. and Canada. We don't own trailers or dispatch a fleet. We manage a vetted carrier network and put it to work for shippers who need a load covered, not a promise.

Every account gets a dedicated rep, not a queue. That's not a policy line, it's how we operate. You work with one person who knows your lanes, your appointment windows, and your exceptions, and who you can actually reach when a load needs attention.

Carriers are vetted before they're ever offered a load, not after something goes wrong. That discipline is what lets us price competitively without cutting corners on service: reliable capacity, delivered on time, at a rate that holds up.
